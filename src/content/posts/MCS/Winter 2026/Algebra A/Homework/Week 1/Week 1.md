- **Problem 1**  
    Show one field axiom that fails for $\mathbb Z_6$, giving a specific example.
    
    - **Solution 1.1**  
      The **multiplicative inverse axiom** fails: every nonzero element of a field must have an inverse.  
      For example, $2\neq 0$ in $\mathbb Z_{6}$, but there no $x\in\mathbb Z_6$ satisfies
      $$
      2x\equiv 1\pmod 6
      $$
      since \(2x\bmod 6\) can only be \(0,2,\) or \(4\).
