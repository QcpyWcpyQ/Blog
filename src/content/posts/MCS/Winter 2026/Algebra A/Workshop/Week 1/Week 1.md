- **Exercise 4**  
    Give examples of $A,B\in M_{3\times 3}(\mathbb R)$ such that  

    1. $AB=0$ and $BA\neq 0$.
    2. $A\neq 0,B\neq 0$ and $AB=BA=0$.

    - **Solution 4.1**  
        1. Let $A=\pmatrix{0&1&0\\0&1&0\\0&1&0}$ and $B=\pmatrix{1&0&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&0}$. Then $AB=0$ and $BA=\pmatrix{0&3&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&0}\neq 0$.
        2. Let $A=\pmatrix{1&0&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&0}\neq 0$ and $B=\pmatrix{0&0&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&0}\neq 0$. Then $AB=BA=0$.
